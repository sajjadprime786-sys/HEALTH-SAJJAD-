🌡️ Fever & Recovery Guide

If you have a fever, focus on staying comfortable, hydrated and rested while monitoring how you feel.

💧 Drink fluidsTake regular drinks to help avoid dehydration.
🛌 RestSleep and avoid workouts or sports while feverish.
🌡️ Check temperatureUse a digital thermometer.
👕 Stay comfortableWear comfortable clothing and avoid excessive blankets.
🍲 Eat if you canChoose normal or easy-to-tolerate foods according to appetite.
🏠 Stay homeRest and avoid spreading illness to others.
🚨 Get medical help urgently if:
you have trouble breathing, severe confusion, are difficult to wake, have a seizure, signs of serious dehydration, blue/grey lips or face, or your condition becomes seriously worse.
💊 Medicines:

Ask a parent/guardian or healthcare professional about appropriate fever medicine and follow the package instructions. Do not take extra doses or combine medicines without checking.
